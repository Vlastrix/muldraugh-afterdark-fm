Muldraugh Afterdark FM's Windows companion bundles the CPython runtime,
Tcl/Tk and the PyInstaller bootloader. Their notices are included in licenses/.

CPython: https://www.python.org/psf/license/
Tcl/Tk: https://www.tcl.tk/software/tcltk/license.html
PyInstaller and bootloader exception: https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/license.html
PyInstaller hooks: https://github.com/pyinstaller/pyinstaller-hooks-contrib

The companion does not bundle Pear Desktop, Project Zomboid, YouTube media,
music files or user credentials. These are independent products.
