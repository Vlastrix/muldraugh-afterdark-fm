# Muldraugh Afterdark FM v0.5.1 [B42.21]

Experimental singleplayer release. Works with Project Zomboid Build 42.21 on Windows, using Pear Desktop (tested with Pear 3.12.0). Multiplayer and other game builds are not verified.

## Downloads and setup

Three parts are required:

1. Subscribe to [Muldraugh Afterdark FM in Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3821956623) and enable the mod in your save. Remove local development copies to avoid duplicates.
2. Install/open [Pear Desktop](https://github.com/pear-devs/pear-desktop), choose a song or playlist, then open **Plugins** and enable the **API Server** plugin included in Pear Desktop. In that plugin's settings, use host `127.0.0.1`, port `26538`, and **Auth at first**.
3. Download **AfterdarkFMConnector-Windows-0.5.1.zip** from this release's assets, extract it and open **AfterdarkFMConnector.exe**. Authorize **PZPearCarRadio** in Pear. Keep Pear and the Connector app open.
4. Enter a car with a working radio and battery. Turn its radio on, select **94.46 MHz Afterdark FM**, press **Tune in**, and unpause the game. Use the normal volume slider and Previous / Pause / Resume / Next buttons.

No Python installation or command prompt is required. The ZIP includes `QUICK_START.txt` and bundled runtime notices. GitHub's automatic source-code downloads are for developers. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers the player ZIP; `QUICK_START.txt` is also attached separately for easy reading.

## Changes in 0.5.1

The Windows app is now named **Afterdark FM Connector** and its executable is `AfterdarkFMConnector.exe`. This is a naming and documentation update; playback behavior is unchanged. Close the previous app before opening Connector. The original v0.5.0 download remains available.

## Included behavior

- Music continues outside the car and fades with distance.
- Playback can attract nearby zombies while the radio is active.
- Switching stations releases control of Pear; disconnecting or closing the Connector pauses controlled playback and restores the prior Pear volume.
- The Windows app shows connection state and track details, with optional folder/port settings.

## Known limits and verification

Singleplayer, Windows, one tracked car. Choose music in Pear; pasting song links in the game is not supported. Audio comes from Pear rather than being streamed by the game. Directional audio and wall/window effects are not simulated. Escape-menu pause behavior needs refinement.

The author confirmed v0.5.0 works with Pear and the subscribed Workshop copy. Version 0.5.1 changes naming and documentation; its renamed executable has passed the packaged GUI startup check, but has not yet been retested by the author in the game. The release build also passed 51 automated tests and a packaged GUI startup check. These checks do not establish multiplayer support or compatibility with untested builds. Installation by a second person is still pending.
