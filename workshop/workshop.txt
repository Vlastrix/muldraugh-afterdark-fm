All players press Start in the Multiplayer radios (Steam) panel. Suppliers also check Allow Use my Pear and select their process before Start. In the powered car radio, tune to Afterdark FM and press Use my Pear. Occupants share Previous, Pause/Resume and Next; another supplier can take over with Use my Pear. Audio uses the existing game connection; no separate Connector VPN or audio port is configured.

No music is downloaded by this mod; multiplayer transmits live captured Pear audio. Restart clients, server and Connector after updating, disable duplicate copies and retune old saved radios to 94.6 MHz. A different free frequency ending in .6 is chosen if 94.6 MHz is occupied.
